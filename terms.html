2. Services Description

ArcticCool provides air conditioning installation, repair, servicing, deep cleaning, gas charging, and annual maintenance contract (AMC) services. All services are performed by certified technicians operating under our supervision. We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any service with appropriate notice.

3. Bookings & Scheduling

  • Service bookings are confirmed via email/SMS within 2 hours of submission
  • We will assign a technician based on availability and proximity
  • We guarantee response within 4 hours in major cities; delays may occur due to exceptional circumstances
  • You must ensure safe access to the AC unit and electrical connections at the time of service
  • Inaccurate location or contact details may result in service cancellation without refund of the visit charge

4. Payments & Pricing

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and inclusive of 18% GST unless stated otherwise
  • Visit charges are payable upfront at the time of booking
  • Repair costs are quoted after diagnosis; customer approval is required before any repair work
  • Payments accepted: Credit/Debit cards, UPI, Net Banking, Razorpay, Cash on completion
  • All online payments are processed through PCI-DSS certified payment gateways

5. Cancellations & Refunds

  • Free cancellation: Cancel more than 4 hours before scheduled service for full refund
  • Late cancellation: Cancellations within 4 hours of service incur a ₹149 cancellation fee
  • No-show: If technician arrives and customer is unavailable, the visit charge is non-refundable
  • Refunds: Processed within 5–7 business days to original payment method
  • Disputes must be raised within 48 hours of service completion

6. AMC Plan Terms

  • AMC plans are annual contracts valid for 12 months from activation date
  • Plans cover one AC unit by default; multi-unit pricing available
  • Service visits must be used within the contract period; unused visits do not carry forward
  • AMC renewal discounts apply only if renewed before expiry date
  • ArcticCool reserves the right to refuse AMC if the AC unit is found to be beyond economical repair

7. Warranties & Guarantees
