Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does AC installation take? +
Most split AC installations are completed within 2–3 hours. Commercial or multi-unit installations may take longer. Our technician will give you an accurate time estimate during the site survey.
What is included in the AMC plan? +
AMC plans include scheduled service visits, deep cleaning, priority emergency support, and discounts on repairs. The Platinum plan includes all repairs, gas refills, and spare parts at no additional cost.
Do you service all AC brands? +
Yes, we service all major brands including Daikin, LG, Samsung, Hitachi, Voltas, Blue Star, O General, Carrier, Godrej, and more. Our technicians are trained on manufacturer-specific systems.
How do I book a service appointment? +
You can book via our website contact form, customer dashboard, or by calling us at +91 98200 12345. We offer same-day and next-day appointments in all major cities.
What is the cost of a gas refill? +
Gas refill (refrigerant recharging) starts from ₹900 for R22 and ₹1,500 for R32/R410A refrigerants. The cost includes leak detection. AMC Gold/Platinum customers get one free refill per year.
Do you provide service at night or on weekends? +
We operate Monday to Saturday from 8 AM to 8 PM, and Sunday from 9 AM to 5 PM. AMC Gold and Platinum subscribers have access to 24/7 emergency support for critical breakdowns.
