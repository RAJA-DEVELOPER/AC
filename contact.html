Quick Answers

Common Questions

How quickly can you send a technician?+
In major cities, we guarantee a technician within 4 hours of booking. Most bookings are fulfilled same-day. AMC Platinum customers have 2-hour priority response.
What are the service charges?+
Service visit starts at ₹299 (waived if you proceed with repair). Installation from ₹1,499. Deep cleaning from ₹599. Gas refill from ₹900. All prices are transparent — no surprises.
Do you offer weekend service?+
Yes! We operate Monday–Saturday 8am–8pm and Sunday 9am–5pm. Platinum AMC subscribers have 24/7 emergency support.
Can I track my technician in real-time?+
Yes! Once your booking is confirmed, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS/email. You can also track via your Customer Dashboard at dashboard.html.
