5 Warning Signs Your AC Needs Immediate Servicing This Summer

Don't wait for a complete breakdown. These early indicators can save you thousands in repair costs.

Vikram Singh
Senior HVAC Engineer, ArcticCool
August 15, 2025
12,847 views
24 comments

Every summer, thousands of air conditioning units across India fail at the worst possible moment — peak heatwave, full house, no technician available for days. The frustrating truth is that most of these failures could have been prevented with early detection and timely maintenance.

As a senior HVAC engineer with over a decade at ArcticCool, I've diagnosed thousands of AC units. In this guide, I'll share the five most reliable early warning signs that your AC is crying out for professional attention.

Quick Tip: Bookmark this page and check your AC against these signs before summer hits. Preventive servicing costs ₹500–₹1,500. Emergency repairs during peak season can cost ₹5,000–₹25,000+.

The 5 Critical Warning Signs

01

Warm or Insufficient Cooling

If your AC runs for 20+ minutes but the room remains warm, it usually signals one of three issues: low refrigerant (gas leak), dirty evaporator coils, or a failing compressor. Don't ignore this — running a gas-starved compressor leads to permanent damage worth ₹8,000–₹20,000 to replace.

02

Unusual Sounds — Rattling, Grinding or Hissing

A healthy AC runs quietly. Rattling suggests loose components or debris in the blower. Grinding indicates worn motor bearings. Hissing is almost always a refrigerant leak — a safety concern that needs immediate attention, as refrigerants can be harmful in enclosed spaces.

03

Water Leaks or Excessive Condensation

A little condensation is normal. But if you see water pooling inside near the indoor unit, it indicates a blocked drainage pipe, frozen evaporator coils, or a refrigerant issue. Left unchecked, this can damage walls, ceiling and electrical components — leading to costly repairs beyond the AC itself.

04

Foul or Musty Odours

Musty smells indicate mould or bacterial growth inside the unit — typically on the evaporator coil or drainage tray. This is a serious air quality concern, especially for children and people with respiratory conditions. A burning smell could indicate electrical issues and requires immediate shutdown and professional inspection.

05

Sudden Spike in Electricity Bills

If your monthly electricity bill increases by 20–30% without changing your usage habits, your AC is likely working harder than it should. Dirty filters, low refrigerant, or failing components force the compressor to overwork — consuming significantly more power while delivering less cooling.

"An AC that's serviced annually costs on average 35% less to run and lasts 4–6 years longer than an unmaintained unit of the same model." — ArcticCool Service Data, 2024

What to Do When You Spot These Signs

Acting quickly when you notice any of these warning signs can mean the difference between a ₹600 filter cleaning and a ₹15,000 compressor replacement. Here's your action plan:

  1. Don't ignore it — the problem will not resolve itself and will worsen with continued use.
  2. Stop running the unit if you hear grinding sounds or notice burning smells.
  3. Call a certified technician within 24–48 hours for a proper diagnosis.
  4. Don't attempt DIY repairs — refrigerant handling requires licensed certification.
  5. Consider an AMC plan for ongoing preventive maintenance and priority service.
Safety Warning: If your AC emits a burning smell or sparks, switch it off immediately at the circuit breaker and call us on +91 98200 12345. Do not attempt to restart it.
