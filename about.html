Our Journey

A Story Built on
Trust & Innovation

What started as a small repair shop has grown into a nationally recognized brand.

2009 — The Beginning

Founded in Mumbai

With just 3 technicians and a second-hand van, ArcticCool began offering residential AC services in Andheri and Bandra.

2012 — First Expansion

Pune & Nashik Launch

After achieving 500+ satisfied customers, we opened our first branch offices and began commercial HVAC projects.

2016 — Digital Transformation

Online Booking Platform

Launched India's first real-time HVAC technician tracking system, revolutionizing the customer experience.

2020 — National Presence

40+ Cities, 500+ Technicians

Expanded operations across India with franchised service centers and ISO 9001:2015 certification.

2025 — Future Ready

AI-Powered Diagnostics

Launched predictive maintenance technology using IoT sensors, keeping your AC healthy before problems arise.
